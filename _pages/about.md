Hi! My name is Jane (she/her) and I'm a 5th year Cognitive Science PhD student at Johns Hopkins University. I am a computational linguist and psycholinguist, working with Kyle Rawlins, Paul Smolensky, and Colin Wilson.

My research focuses on the learning and real-time processing of linguistic representations. How do we learn these complex representations with little data, and how are they accessed/built as we plan out our speech? I use computational modelling and behavioural experiments to answer these questions. My specialty lies in morphology and phonology, though I also work in other linguistic domains. My current work is generously supported by a SSHRC Doctoral Fellowship.
